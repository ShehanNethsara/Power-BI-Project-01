🚚SwiftRoute  Logistics and Supply Chain Management Analytics Dashboard

📝 Project Overview

SwiftRoute Logistics is a data-driven Power BI solution designed to optimize end-to-end delivery operations. The dashboard provides actionable insights into supply chain bottlenecks, fleet reliability, and workforce efficiency. By analyzing over 4 major data domains—Orders, Hubs, Drivers, and Vehicles—this project helps stakeholders reduce delays and enhance customer satisfaction.

🎯 Key Business Objectives

Operational Efficiency: Monitor On-Time Delivery (OTD) rates and analyze root causes for delays.
Resource Optimization: Evaluate Hub capacity vs. actual utilization to prevent bottlenecks.
Fleet Management: Track vehicle health, age-to-breakdown correlations, and maintenance needs.
Performance Benchmarking: Rank hubs and drivers based on KPIs like CSAT and delivery speed.

🛠️ Tech Stack

Visualization: Power BI Desktop
Calculations: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for time-intelligence and MoM growth metrics.
Data Modeling: Star Schema (Connecting Orders, Hubs, Drivers, and Vehicles).

📊 Dashboard Architecture

The solution is divided into four specialized views:

1. Executive Overview
   
  High-Level KPIs: Total Orders, On-Time Delivery Rate (%), CSAT %, and Avg Delivery Time.
  Growth Tracking: Month-over-Month (MoM) performance comparisons.

3. Hub Operations Insights
   
  Capacity Analysis: Identification of over-utilized vs. under-utilized hubs.
  Processing Efficiency: Matrix analysis of daily hub processing times.

4. Driver Performance Analytics
   
  Correlation: Scatter plots mapping Driver Experience vs. Performance Ratings.
  Accountability: Identifying drivers with the highest delay frequencies for targeted coaching.

5. Fleet & Vehicle Health
   
  Risk Assessment: Analyzing the relationship between vehicle age and breakdown frequency.
  Utilization: Distribution of orders across different vehicle models and types.

📂 Data Schema & Knowledge Base

The project utilizes a robust relational data model:
Orders Table: Tracks delivery status, timestamps, and customer feedback.
Hubs Table: Contains operational capacity and location data.
Drivers Table: Stores employment types, experience years, and ratings.
Vehicles Table: Tracks maintenance history, status, and breakdown alerts.

🚀 Key Insights for Stakeholders

Late Deliveries: Identified specific "Hub Bottlenecks" where processing time exceeded the 4-hour threshold.
Reliability: Vehicles older than X years showed a 30% higher breakdown rate, suggesting a need for fleet renewal.
Quality Control: High correlation found between driver experience (5+ years) and top-tier CSAT scores.
